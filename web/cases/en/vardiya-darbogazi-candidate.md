# Koru Distribution — Daily operations plan

Original synthetic practice case · 3.0.0 · Level 1 · About 60 minutes

Koru Distribution’s outbound list for today is final. The last truck leaves at 17:30. The shift supervisor is on leave, so you will run today’s work allocation and the dispatch meeting.

## Candidate task

You are expected to prepare and submit the requested outputs based on the company file below. The decision and the working approach are yours.

## D01 / Company file / Koru Distribution

Since 2018, Koru Distribution has prepared orders for four home and office product brands in a single centre. The centre has 96 employees; on the shift in this case, 11 people are assigned to picking and packing. Other teams handle goods receipt, stock and loading. The company’s performance meeting is based on daily dispatch and customer feedback.

The small-item and large-item areas are physically separate. The packing area is the shared exit for both. Staff training records are kept by the shift supervisor. Order volume has changed over the last two weeks because of a campaign. Teams plan the day with standard times; actual end-of-day records are entered in a separate system. Task changes during the shift are announced on the board and in company messaging. Today’s outbound order list was finalised at 08:00. Changing the truck time depends on carrier and customer approval.

## D08 / Internal messages / before the meeting

## D09 / Operating rules and meeting records

The shift starts at 08:30. The net 300 minutes is the time left after each person’s breaks and planned activities. The interim targets are not a customer priority list; contract-based customer exceptions are kept at the carrier desk. On the end-of-day handover form, dispatched orders, waiting orders and orders held by quality are in separate fields.
